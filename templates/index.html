About the model

This is a Logistic Regression Model used to predict whether monthly returns on an index is positive or negative. Historical data is pulled in through Yahoo Finance (yfinance) and cleaned/evaluated with Pandas. The models outputs either a 0 (negative monthly return) or a 1 (positive monthly return)
